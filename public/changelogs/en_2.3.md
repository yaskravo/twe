The first Expert release. Here is what's new compared to Telegram Web K.

• Deleted messages stay. If someone deletes a message, you still see it in the chat with a mark next to the time. It's kept only on this device. You can read it, copy it or delete it from the device.
• A new look. Rounder bubbles and menus, warmer colors, the Nunito font and an orange accent. You can switch back to classic Telegram at any time.
• Lots of settings in Settings → Expert: chat background (ready-made ones with their own accent color, or your own color or photo with an accent you pick), corner roundness, font (built in, from your computer or your own file) and how deleted messages look.
• Light mode for older computers. It turns off heavy effects so everything stays smooth. On automatic it turns on by itself when your computer needs it.
• Ghost mode. Nobody sees that you read their messages, type, are online or watch their stories. Turn it on and off right from the side menu.
